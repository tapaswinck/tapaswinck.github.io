About

I am a recent graduate in Physics and Mathematics from Washington College (GPA 3.689/4.00), with research experience spanning high-energy particle physics, astrophysics, and computational engineering. My work has included a study of likelihood methods for Beyond-the-Standard-Model Higgs-boson searches with the US ATLAS collaboration, an analysis of proton-proton collision data from the ATLAS detector at CERN, and the validation of Cosmic Inflation models against Planck satellite data.

Alongside this research, I have built systems-level and applied engineering experience: deploying a ten-node high-performance computing cluster, and designing and building an electric propulsion system for a competition sailing vessel. More recently, my independent work has extended into machine learning, including physics-informed neural networks, variational autoencoders, and Bayesian optimisation applied to aerospace design problems.

I am currently based in India and am open to research, data science, and engineering roles.
